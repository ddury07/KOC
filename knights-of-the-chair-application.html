Requirements and Responsibilities
  1. The applicant must be a practicing Catholic male student at St. Charles Preparatory School.
  2. The applicant understands that the Knights of the Chair are specially selected to serve at Liturgies for the Bishop of Columbus. While this is a position of honor, the server must exhibit modesty in speech when discussing this service with others.
  3. The applicant must be striving to be docile, humble, and disciplined in the life of Christian virtue.
  4. The applicant must possess a friendly and brotherly attitude.
  5. The applicant must be willing to help his peers to serve well at the Altar.
  6. The applicant must be generous with his time to serve the Bishop at Saint Joseph Cathedral for particular Masses. (Time commitments will vary with the availability of each Knight, with the minimum of serving one or two Masses per month. There is no expectation that a student will miss classes.)
  7. Transportation to the Cathedral is to be arranged by each Knight for himself.
  8. The applicant must be committed to attend Knights of the Chair meetings, training sessions, rehearsals, and other occasional events and to serve at Masses on a rotational basis.
