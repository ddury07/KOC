Altar Server Experience

Tap or hover the ⓘ next to each role for a short explanation.

General altar assistant who helps at the altar: preparing the vessels, presenting the water and wine, washing the celebrant’s hands, ringing the bells, and assisting with the sacred vessels. In some settings this is the primary “server” role.
Carries a lighted processional candle, usually flanking the crucifer in the entrance and exit processions and accompanying the Gospel procession. May also assist with other sanctuary duties.
Carries the processional cross (crucifix) at the head of the entrance and exit processions (or immediately behind the thurifer when incense is used). Often also serves as book-bearer at certain moments.
Holds the Roman Missal (or Book of the Chair) open for the bishop or priest so he can proclaim the presidential prayers with both hands free. Also moves the book between the chair and the altar as needed.
Carries the thurible (incense burner) and often the incense boat. Leads processions when incense is used, presents the thurible to the priest/bishop for imposing incense, and may incense the altar, gifts, or people.
Holds the bishop’s crozier (pastoral staff) and/or miter (pointed hat) when the bishop is not wearing or carrying them. Often wears a vimpa (veil) so the insignia are not touched with bare hands. These roles are used only when a bishop is present.

Requirements and Responsibilities
  1. The applicant must be a practicing Catholic male student at St. Charles Preparatory School.
  2. The applicant understands that the Knights of the Chair are specially selected to serve at Liturgies for the Bishop of Columbus. While this is a position of honor, the server must exhibit modesty in speech when discussing this service with others.
  3. The applicant must be striving to be docile, humble, and disciplined in the life of Christian virtue.
  4. The applicant must possess a friendly and brotherly attitude.
  5. The applicant must be willing to help his peers to serve well at the Altar.
  6. The applicant must be generous with his time to serve the Bishop at Saint Joseph Cathedral for particular Masses. (Time commitments will vary with the availability of each Knight, with the minimum of serving one or two Masses per month. There is no expectation that a student will miss classes.)
  7. Transportation to the Cathedral is to be arranged by each Knight for himself.
  8. The applicant must be committed to attend Knights of the Chair meetings, training sessions, rehearsals, and other occasional events and to serve at Masses on a rotational basis.
